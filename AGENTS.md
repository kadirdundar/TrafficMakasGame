# Notes for AI agents

- Adding or fixing a car model (exterior GLB, cockpit GLB, lights, cluster, seating position):
  read `docs/3D-ARAC-MODELI-TARIFI.md` first and follow it. The Audi A8 is the reference quality.
- Verify visual changes with `tools/model-preview/` screenshots (see section 6 of that document)
  before calling them done.
